🕒 Classes & timings
Classes run on Saturdays during term time. Ages 5–7 meet 9:00–10:00 am, Ages 8–10 meet 10:15–11:15 am, and Ages 11–13 meet 11:30–12:30 pm.
Children are grouped by age — 5–7, 8–10 and 11–13 — so sessions are pitched just right for every stage. Groups are small (maximum 15–20 children) for lots of participation and support.
Sessions are held in Solihull. The exact venue is being confirmed and will be shared with registered families ahead of the first session.
📚 Curriculum & activities
Children explore Hindu beliefs and traditions, stories from the Ramayana and Mahabharata, simple teachings from the Bhagavad Gita, Hindu festivals and their meanings, and core values such as kindness, respect, honesty, courage and gratitude.
Every week includes a Hindu story, a Bhagavad Gita teaching, a value to practise, a fun activity or craft, and a take-home challenge to enjoy with the family.
