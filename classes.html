What your child will learn

A rich, age-appropriate programme

Our carefully planned programme introduces children to many areas of Hindu life:

🕉️ Hindu Gods & Goddesses 📖 Ramayana & Mahabharata 📜 Bhagavad Gita 🪔 Diwali, Holi, Janmashtami & Navratri 🙏 Sanskrit prayers & shlokas 🧘 Yoga & mindfulness 🌬️ Meditation & breathing ❤️ Hindu values 👵 Respect for parents & elders ☸️ Karma & Dharma 🛕 Temples & worship 🔱 Hindu symbols 🌿 Vegetarianism & Ahimsa ⭐ Confidence, gratitude & leadership
