🌟 About us

Rooted in culture, growing in confidence

Dharmic Stars is a warm, safe community where children aged 5+ connect with Sanatana Dharma and their Hindu heritage through joy — not pressure.

Our mission

Our mission is simple: to create a welcoming, affordable and inspiring community where children can develop a lifelong connection with Hinduism. We help children learn about Sanatana Dharma, understand Hindu beliefs and traditions, build confidence in their identity, and develop kindness, compassion, respect and gratitude.

We believe Hindu education should be engaging, practical and enjoyable — not simply memorising facts. Children learn best through stories, discussion, games, crafts, yoga, music, celebrations and interactive activities.

Our teaching

Classes are led by our founder Rishikaji — a Level 3 Teaching Assistant and qualified yoga teacher of 17+ years — who makes learning playful and welcoming for every child. Groups are kept small so everyone can take part, ask questions and get individual support.
