🌟 About us

Rooted in culture, growing in confidence

Dharmic Stars is a warm, safe Saturday space where children aged 5–13 connect with their Hindu heritage through joy — not pressure.

Our mission

We help children build knowledge, confidence and strong values inspired by Hinduism. Through stories, festivals, the Bhagavad Gita and hands-on activities, we make our beautiful culture come alive — so children feel proud of who they are and where they come from.

Every session mixes learning with fun: a Hindu story, a Gita teaching, a value to practise, a creative activity, and a take-home challenge to share with the family.

Our teaching

Sessions are led by an experienced, enthusiastic teacher who makes learning playful and welcoming for every child. Groups are kept small (maximum 15–20 children) so everyone can take part, ask questions and get individual support.
