Why Dharmic Stars was created

Born from a parent's wish

Like many parents, I realised my own children were learning very little about Hinduism at school. Although schools teach Religious Education, Hinduism often receives only limited attention — and I wanted my children to appreciate the deeper meaning behind our beliefs, traditions and values, not just festivals and facts.

As family life became busier, finding time to teach everything at home became harder. So rather than accept that, I started running weekly Hindu learning camps — and the response was overwhelming. Parents, grandparents and children all asked the same thing: "When can we come back? Can we do this every week?"

Those wonderful responses inspired the creation of Dharmic Stars.

Our mission

A lifelong connection with Hinduism

A welcoming, affordable and inspiring community where every child can grow. Each class is designed to help children:

💪

Feel proud and confident in their cultural identity and heritage.

❤️

We believe Hindu education should be engaging, practical and enjoyable — children learn best through stories, discussion, games, crafts, yoga, music and celebrations.

💛 Community initiative

Affordable for every family

Dharmic Stars is a community initiative — not a profit-driven organisation. We keep our fees affordable so as many families as possible can benefit. If finances are ever difficult, please just talk to us — we're always happy to discuss flexible options, because no child should miss out on learning about their faith.

Donations help us provide learning resources, books, arts & crafts, rewards and certificates, festival celebrations and community events. Every gift, large or small, enriches the experience for every child.
