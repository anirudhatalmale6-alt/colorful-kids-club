About Dharmic Stars

A welcoming space to explore Hinduism

Dharmic Stars is a warm, welcoming space where children explore Hinduism in a fun, engaging and meaningful way. Through stories, traditions and activities, we help children build a strong connection to their culture, values and identity.

Our classes are led by an experienced, DBS-checked teacher and kept small — so every child feels supported, included and confident.
