🛡️ Safeguarding & Child Protection
The safety and wellbeing of children is our highest priority. Children are supervised throughout every session, staff and volunteers understand their safeguarding responsibilities, appropriate checks (including DBS where required) are completed, and any concerns are recorded and dealt with promptly. Children are always encouraged to speak to a trusted adult.
We have a Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) and a Deputy DSL who oversee child protection. If you ever have a concern about a child's safety or wellbeing, please speak to the DSL. (Named contacts will be confirmed with registered families.)
Our safeguarding policy is developed in line with UK safeguarding legislation and guidance, including the Children Act 1989 & 2004, Working Together to Safeguard Children, the Equality Act 2010, UK GDPR and NSPCC guidance. Safeguarding is a shared responsibility, and we work with parents, carers and relevant agencies whenever needed.
⛑️ Health & Safety
We check rooms before each session, keep walkways and fire exits clear, arrange furniture safely and follow all venue safety requirements. Children are supervised at all times and are never left unsupervised.
Yes. We complete risk assessments for our activities and venue, considering potential hazards, who may be affected, the level of risk and the control measures needed — including any additional needs of children. Assessments are reviewed regularly and whenever activities, staffing or the venue change.
We ensure access to first aid supplies and a responsible adult who knows the first aid arrangements. All accidents requiring first aid are recorded, and parents/carers are informed of any significant injury or illness.
🔒 Data Protection & Privacy (UK GDPR)
Only what we need to keep your child safe and run the club — your child's name and age, parent/carer contact details, emergency contacts, and any medical, allergy or additional-needs information. Children's data is treated with particular care.
Records are stored securely with access restricted to authorised individuals, electronic records are password protected, and we avoid unnecessary duplication. We only collect what is necessary, keep it accurate and up to date, and never sell, rent or share personal information for marketing.
Only when it's necessary to protect a child, when legally required, or to provide safe services — for example sharing with local authority safeguarding teams, police or emergency services in a safeguarding situation. Photography and optional communications are always based on your consent.
📘 Parent Handbook highlights
Respect, kindness, responsibility, learning and community. We help children understand Hindu beliefs, traditions and festivals, build confidence in their cultural identity, and make positive friendships — in a safe, respectful and engaging environment.
Children should arrive on time, ready to take part. Children are only released to authorised adults, and cannot leave independently unless written permission has been provided and agreed. Please let us know promptly of any change to collection arrangements.
We want Dharmic Stars to be a happy, respectful place for everyone. Children are asked to listen to adults, respect other children, take part positively and look after resources. Kindness and respect are at the heart of everything we do.
